07 · Evidence and its limits

More evidence.
Not a final verdict.

  • Neural prediction

    Brain patterns

    Five fMRI participants: category-related patterns, not 27 hardwired modules.

  • 2,474 languages

    Language

    Concept meanings vary, alongside shared affective structure.

  • Expression in context

    Interpretation

    Shared face–context patterns do not reveal a person’s private feeling.

These findings constrain theories. They do not settle innateness, universality, or a single “best” taxonomy.

    Explicit controls such as anxiety: 0.72. Useful for agent memory, avatars, and UI.

  • Learned embeddings

09 · A complementary architecture

Learned features.
Inspectable controls.

  1. 01 · Observe

    Context

    Keep observations and their provenance separate from the designed state.

  2. 02 · Represent

    Features

    Use a versioned latent vector when perception or retrieval requires it.

  3. 03 · Expose

    Controls

    Retain named [0, 1] channels for deliberate behavior and inspection.

Validate any mapping. Confidence is not intensity, and a larger embedding is not automatically a more complete account of emotion.
