01 / Scope

This is engineering exploration, not source study

The research packs explain source ideas: cognitive routing, memory systems, valuation and gating, affect, and latent representation. This practical track does something different: it calibrates a model-specific readout, tests quantized transfer, and prepares a later steering experiment.

Two tracks

Source exploration gives the vocabulary and hypotheses; practical experiments test whether those hypotheses survive contact with a model. The 27 channels are a model-derived projection, not a validated semantic feeling system.

02 / Readout v1

What the Qwen 3.5 9B readout shows

The readout peaks around decoder layer 14. BF16, bitsandbytes 8-bit, and Q6-dequantized readout are nearly identical, but gate and intensity best layers shift across backends: BF16 gate 23, 8-bit gate 19, Q6-dequantized gate 28; BF16 intensity 23, 8-bit intensity 19, Q6-dequantized intensity 23.

04 / Next

Robustness complete; next is bounded steering

Backend decision: BF16 is the reference geometry, bitsandbytes 8-bit is the deployment candidate, and Q6-dequantized is a weight-transfer check only.

Robustness checks show mean pooling is better than last-token pooling, lexical false positives are low, and isotonic intensity improves active-row calibration. Cross-validation is lower than the single split, so the readout is exploratory but not yet control-grade.

The next phase is experiments/steering/v1/ with activation addition, random-direction controls, dose-response, reversibility, coherence, and safety checks.

Steering must be tested separately from readout. Read-best is not steer-best.
