03 · The triple-network framework

Three contributions.
Not three isolated minds.

  • Default-mode · DMN

    Context

    Memory, self-related processing, social inference, and meaning.

  • Executive / control

    Regulation

    Maintain and manipulate task information; organize and evaluate plans.

  • Salience · SN

    Relevance

    Contribute to detecting important events and changing network engagement.

These are broad orientations. Network boundaries and names differ across studies; none has one exclusive job.

04 · Read the measurement first

A signal is not an on/off switch.

  • Activity

    Relative change

    Activation and deactivation are measured against a baseline or comparison.

  • Connectivity

    Related variation

    Signals vary together—or oppositely. Correlation alone does not give causal direction.

  • Directionality

    Added prediction

    Directed analyses ask whether one signal helps predict another. That is not the same as perturbing it.

fMRI measures blood-oxygen-related signals. Implanted-electrode recordings offer a different temporal view. Neither measures the whole brain without limitations.

05 · Where the switch metaphor came from

A coordinating hub.
A switching hypothesis.

  • Observe a relevant event. Auditory boundaries and visual oddballs change the pattern of task-related activity.
  • Estimate timing and directed interactions. The right fronto-insular region emerges as an outflow hub in the modeled network.
  • Interpret the coordination. The region may help initiate changes in network engagement—not act as a proven universal CPU.

The 2008 fMRI study used timing and Granger analyses, not direct manipulation of the proposed hub. Menon’s 2011 paper is a broader review/framework.

07 · Finer maps, careful labels

“A” and “B” are not one universal map.

  • Control network · Dixon 2018

    FPCN-A / B

    Relative coupling preferences for default versus dorsal-attention networks. Preferences remain context-sensitive.

  • Default territory · DiNicola 2020

    Network A / B

    Relative task preferences for episodic projection versus theory of mind, resolved through individual mapping.

These subdivide different systems. Neither implies exclusive functions, and neither can simply be merged with a separate default-network core/subsystem account.

08 · Intracranial evidence

Faster recordings.
Bounded conclusions.

  • Das & Menon · 2020

    Rest / intervals

    Directed SN outflow patterns in two clinical datasets—not an encoding-versus-recall experiment.

  • Das & Menon · 2024

    Memory tasks

    Anterior-insula-directed influence during encoding and recall across four experiments.

  • Interpretation

    Not a scheduler

    Phase transfer entropy estimates directed dependence. It does not prove universal continuous switching.

The 2024 authors explicitly identify causal-manipulation and rapid-dynamics limitations. Clinical electrode coverage also constrains inference.

09 · The default network beyond idling

Make information meaningful.

  • Connect experience. Bring memory and personally relevant knowledge into current understanding.
  • Interpret context. Contribute to semantic, social, and narrative processing—not only stimulus-free thought.
  • Investigate integration. An ongoing internal narrative is a unifying proposal, not one proven function of every DMN operation.

Listening to a story can draw on the DMN. Similar responses to a shared narrative do not guarantee agreement or truth.
